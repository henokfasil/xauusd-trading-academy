How to reason
  • HH / HL = each new high/low is above the last one of its kind → uptrend.
  • LH / LL = each new high/low is below the last one of its kind → downtrend.
  • Compare a high to the previous high, and a low to the previous low — never a high to a low.
  • A break of structure (e.g. a LL in an uptrend) is your first warning the trend may be shifting.
