A Day Trading XAU/USD

A stage-by-stage walkthrough of a professional's day. Expand each stage for what you're doing, what you're looking at, the question you're answering, and what NOT to do.

These times are illustrative (roughly London time), not fixed rules. Session times and the London/NY overlap shift with daylight saving — which changes on different dates in Europe and the US — so the relationships drift by an hour a few times a year. Learn the rhythm (quiet Asia → London expansion → NY/overlap), then re-anchor to your own platform clock.

What am I doing?

Open Daily → 4H → 1H. Read the trend and structure top-down before any lower-timeframe noise.

What am I looking at?

Sequence of highs/lows (HH/HL vs LH/LL), the most recent decisive move, and where the Daily/4H closed yesterday.

What question am I answering?

What is my bias today — bullish, bearish, or range — and how strong is it?

What should I NOT do?

Don't skip to the 1-minute chart. Don't form a bias from a single candle.

Example chart situation

4H making higher highs and higher lows, price pulling back → bias bullish, I'll favour longs from support.

Beginner mistake

Deciding direction from the 5m because it 'looks like it's going up' right now.

Professional thought process

Write the bias down in one sentence and let it filter every trade for the rest of the day.
